Run the whole shebang:
python3 clippy.py


Or run individual scripts:

Extract clip from full show:
python3 clip.py show.mov 00:10:10.5 00:11:44

Crop horizontally:
python3 crop.py clip.mov 20 60

Generate Captions, first pass:
python3 caption.py cropped.mov

Generate Captions, after manually updating captions.srt:
python3 caption.py cropped.mov --srt captions.srt

Remove anything you don't want:
python3 remove.py captioned.mov 00:00:03.5 00:00:18

If needed for sharing, make file under 25mb:
python3 smallify.py captioned.mov
